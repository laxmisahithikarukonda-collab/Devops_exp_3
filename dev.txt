Established in 2001, Sridevi Women's Engineering College (SWEC) is an autonomous,
NAAC A++ rated and NBA-accredited women's engineering institution in Hyderabad, Telangana.
Located near Wipro in Gopanpally/Gandipet,
it is affiliated with JNTU Hyderabad and offers undergraduate and postgraduate tech and management programs.
Admissions: Undergrad admissions primarily run through the Telangana TS EAMCET counseling process, and MBA selection requires TSICET.
Facilities: Sprawling green campus, well-stocked central library, modern laboratories, on-campus hostel and bus transit networks, 
and training associations via TASK (Telangana Academy for Skill and Knowledge).
